Frequently Asked Questions

Which blockchain is VROX built on?
Vrox Network is built on the high-performance Solana Blockchain, providing lightning-fast transactions and near-zero fees for our global community.
What is the total supply of $VROX?
The total supply is fixed at 20,000,000,000 (20 Billion) $VROX, strictly capped to ensure scarcity and long-term value retention.
Is KYC mandatory for withdrawals?
Yes, mandatory KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is required for all withdrawals to ensure a bot-free environment and maintain the integrity of our decentralized network.
What is the "Verify & Purge" document policy?
We protect your privacy. Once your identity is successfully verified for Solana withdrawals, all uploaded ID images and sensitive documents are permanently deleted from our secure servers.
What happens to unmined tokens?
Upon the Token Generation Event (TGE), all remaining unmined supply from the community reward pool will be Immediately and Permanently Burned.
Can I withdraw to any Solana address?
Yes! Once withdrawals are enabled in Phase 4 of our roadmap, you will be able to transfer your VROX to any external Solana address, including CEXs, DEXs, or private wallets.
How to start mining?
Go to the Mine screen in the app and tap 'START MINING'. Your session will last for 24 hours, after which you need to restart it.
Is Vrox Network mining free?
Yes, cloud mining on Vrox Network is completely free and doesn't consume your phone's hardware resources or battery.
Are there forced ads in Vrox Network?
No! We are strictly against intrusive advertisements. Vrox Network is a Forced-Ad Free Zone. Any advertisement you see is 100% voluntary and rewards you with extra bonuses.

If you couldn't find the answer to your question in our FAQs, feel free to reach out to us. We're here to help!
