VROX Help Center

How can we help?

Project & Blockchain

Which blockchain is VROX built on?
Vrox Network is built on the high-performance Solana Blockchain. We chose Solana for its incredible speed (65,000+ TPS) and near-zero transaction fees, ensuring a seamless experience for our Pioneers.
What is the total supply of $VROX?
The total supply is strictly fixed at 20,000,000,000 (20 Billion) $VROX. This cap ensures scarcity and supports long-term value within our ecosystem.
What is the "Massive Token Burn" protocol?
Upon our Mainnet launch and Token Generation Event (TGE), all unmined tokens remaining in the community pool will be permanently burned. This effectively reduces supply and rewards early active miners.
Is the team dumping tokens?
No. To ensure project stability, the 15% Team allocation is subject to a 12-month cliff and 4-year linear vesting. The team cannot sell tokens during the critical early stages of the project.

Mining & Earnings

How to start mining?
Download the app, register with your Google account, and tap the 'MINE' button. Your session will last for 24 hours, after which you simply need to restart it with one tap.
Does mining drain my battery?
No. Vrox Network uses Proprietary Cloud Mining Virtualization. All heavy work happens on our secure servers, meaning zero impact on your battery, CPU, or hardware.
How can I boost my mining rate?
You can boost your rate in three ways: 1) Invite friends for a 25% permanent boost per active ref. 2) Use the 'Boost' feature for a temporary 10% multiplier. 3) Complete Quests and play mini-games.
What are Referral Elite rewards?
Top community builders can participate in our Referral Campaigns to win real USDT rewards and exclusive Pioneer status badges.
What is the "Early Bird" status?
The first 10,000 members to join Vrox Network receive the Early Bird Adoptor tag, which grants exclusive access to future rewards and specialized community features.

Security & KYC

Is KYC mandatory for withdrawals?
Yes. Mandatory KYC verification is required before any withdrawal to external Solana addresses. This prevents bot manipulation and ensures a fair, human-only network.
What is the "Verify & Purge" document policy?
We prioritize your data sovereignty. Once your identity is successfully verified, all uploaded ID images and sensitive documents are permanently deleted from our servers within 24 hours.
How is my data secured?
We use Supabase with military-grade AES-256 encryption for all user data. Your progress is synced across devices and backed up in a secure, isolated cloud environment.
Are there forced ads in the app?
No. Vrox Network is a Forced-Ad Free Zone. We never interrupt your experience with unexpected pop-ups. Any ad you see is 100% voluntary and grants you extra VROX bonuses.
Can I use multiple devices?
No. To maintain fairness, our policy is One Account Per Person. Using cloning apps or multiple devices to mine will result in a permanent ban and loss of all tokens.

Withdrawals & Roadmap

When can I withdraw my tokens?
Withdrawals will be enabled in Phase 4 of our milestone-based roadmap, following the Token Generation Event (TGE) and Solana Mainnet launch.
Where can I withdraw to?
You will be able to withdraw your $VROX to any external Solana address, including major exchanges (CEX), decentralized exchanges (DEX), or private wallets like Phantom.
How is the token price calculated?
The price is dynamic and increases based on the total tokens mined globally. As our community grows and supply is distributed, the scarcity model drives potential value.
What if I lose my phone?
Simply download the app on your new device and sign in with your verified Google account. Your balance and mining progress are cloud-synced and will be recovered instantly.
How do I report a technical bug?
You can use the 'Report a Problem' card in the app's Support screen or email us directly at support@vroxnetwork.com with a screenshot of the issue.
