Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

Our Security Philosophy

At VROX Network, we believe that decentralized empowerment starts with individual privacy. Our infrastructure is engineered to be as secure as the Solana blockchain itself. We utilize Supabase with industry-leading AES-256 encryption to ensure your account data remains confidential and inaccessible to unauthorized parties.

Information Collection

To provide a high-integrity ecosystem for our Pioneers, we collect the following limited information:

  • Identity Data: Full Name and Country for regional community distribution and analytics.
  • Authentication: Google Sign-In details are used to provide a secure and passwordless login experience.
  • Mining Activity: Real-time session data to accurately credit your VROX rewards.
  • KYC Documents: For Pioneers reaching the withdrawal threshold, we collect ID verification documents.

The "Verify & Purge" Commitment

VROX Network is committed to protecting your sensitive documents. Our Verify & Purge policy ensures that your privacy is never compromised:

"Once your identity is successfully verified for Solana withdrawals, all uploaded ID images and sensitive documents are automatically and permanently deleted from our secure servers."

We only retain the 'Verified' status on your profile. We do not store your private documents longer than absolutely necessary for the verification process.

Advertising & Analytics

We use Google AdMob to support the development of our ecosystem. AdMob may collect your device's unique Advertising ID and IP address to prevent fraudulent activity and deliver relevant community-based announcements. VROX Network never sells your personal information to third-party data brokers.

Account Deletion Rights

You have full sovereignty over your data. You can request account deletion directly within the app settings. Upon deletion, all your personal records, including profile data and mined balances, are permanently purged from the Supabase database within 24 hours.

Contact Our Security Team

If you have any questions regarding your data sovereignty or our security protocols, please reach out to: support@vroxnetwork.com
